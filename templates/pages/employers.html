What engagement models does GCTT support?

W2 contract staffing, contract-to-hire with an agreed evaluation period, direct-hire search, and defined project teams. Rates, conversion terms and billing schedules are agreed before work starts.

How does GCTT assess technical qualifications?

An intake covering communication, work authorisation and project history, followed by a technical conversation with a practitioner in the relevant stack. The scorecard is agreed with you before screening.

What turnaround should we expect?

Standard technical roles target a shortlist within 48–72 business hours. Niche SAP mandates typically take 3–5 business days for an initial pool. The target is confirmed at calibration.

What replacement terms are available?

Direct-hire placements carry a 90-day replacement warranty. Contract engagements include a trial period; if the practitioner is not the right fit, GCTT replaces them and does not bill the trial hours. Exact terms are set in the engagement letter.
