Frequently Asked Enterprise Questions

Practical details on engagement structures, turnaround SLAs, and quality guarantees.

What engagement models does GCTT support?

We provide flexible models tailored to your project budgets: Direct W2 Contract Staffing, Contract-to-Hire (typically 3–6 months trial periods), and Direct-Hire Executive Search. All engagement terms, billing rates, and conversion schedules are transparently agreed upfront.

How does GCTT vet technical qualifications?

Every candidate completes a two-stage screening process: initial recruiter intake covering communication, work authorization, and project history, followed by an in-depth technical evaluation assessing hands-on architectural competence in the required tech stack (e.g., S/4HANA, Java/Spring, Cloud AWS/Azure).

What is the standard turnaround time for candidate shortlists?

For standard technical roles (Full Stack, DevOps, Scrum Masters), vetted candidate profiles are submitted within 48 to 72 business hours. For niche enterprise mandates (such as SAP MM/FICO architects), initial candidate pools are delivered within 3 to 5 business days.

What satisfaction and replacement guarantees are provided?

All contracts include a quality guarantee period. If a consultant does not meet project standards within the agreed initial assessment window, billing is waived for that initial evaluation period and an expedited replacement is sourced immediately.
