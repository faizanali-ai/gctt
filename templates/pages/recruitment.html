GCTT runs a defined search rather than a job-board filter. We agree the scorecard, map the market, assess against the role, and stay involved through the offer.

The search process

  1. 01

    Calibration

    Role outcomes, non-negotiables, compensation range and the interview process are agreed in writing.

  2. 02

    Market mapping

    Targeted, discreet outreach to practitioners who have already done the work.

  3. 03

    Technical assessment

    A structured conversation on architecture, trade-offs and delivery history, documented for your review.

  4. 04

    Shortlist and offer

    A short, comparable shortlist with availability and compensation expectations. Replacement terms are agreed in the engagement letter.
