DELIVERY GOVERNANCE

Services & Engagement FAQ

Clear answers on enterprise delivery models, compliance guarantees, and workforce onboarding.

How are GCTT's service practices structured for enterprise delivery?

We operate specialized vertical practices across Technical Staffing, Workforce Upskilling, and Enterprise Cloud Solutions. Clients can engage via targeted staff augmentation, project-based pods, or comprehensive workforce transformation programs with dedicated account managers and technical practice leads.

What verification is performed prior to candidate submission?

Every candidate completes a rigorous 3-tier validation: (1) identity and Federal E-Verify right-to-work clearance, (2) live sandbox technical defense evaluated by practicing enterprise architects, and (3) reference validation from past direct engineering supervisors.

Can training programmes be customized for existing enterprise engineering teams?

Yes. Our academy practice crafts dedicated private corporate cohorts aligned with your internal tech stacks, migration roadmaps, and delivery schedules. Inquire via our corporate partnerships team.
