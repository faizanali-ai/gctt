Services & Engagement FAQ

Clear answers on enterprise delivery models, compliance guarantees, and workforce onboarding.

How are GCTT's service practices structured for enterprise delivery?

We operate specialized vertical practices across Technical Staffing, Workforce Upskilling, and Enterprise Cloud Solutions. Clients can engage via targeted staff augmentation, project-based pods, or comprehensive workforce transformation programs with dedicated account managers and technical practice leads.

What compliance and work authorization standards are maintained?

All talent placed or trained through GCTT undergoes rigorous right-to-work verification (E-Verify, Form I-9, criminal background check, and education/credential validation). We adhere strictly to US federal and state labor regulations and provide client-audit-ready documentation packages.

Can training programs be customized for internal enterprise teams?

Yes. In addition to our public cohort curricula, GCTT develops private corporate cohorts tailored to enterprise architectures—such as migrating legacy ERP to SAP S/4HANA or transitioning traditional waterfall teams to Scaled Agile (SAFe) delivery frameworks.

What locations and time zones do your technical consultants support?

Headquartered in Irving, Texas, GCTT supports on-site, hybrid, and remote deployments across all US time zones (Eastern, Central, Mountain, Pacific), ensuring seamless synchronous collaboration with client engineering and delivery leadership.
