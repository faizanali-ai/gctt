Delivery governance

Questions we get asked.

How are the practices structured?

Staffing, recruitment, talent acquisition and the SAP/Scrum practice are separate engagements. A client can use one or combine them, and the commercial terms stay with each engagement.

What compliance checks are completed?

GCTT is an E-Verify employer. Right-to-work verification, Form I-9 and the checks agreed for the specific role are completed before placement. Background and credential checks are scoped with the client.

Can the Academy train an internal team?

Yes. Public cohorts run on a published schedule; corporate cohorts are scoped to the team's stack and delivery process. Both lead to a verifiable completion certificate.
