GCTT works with three groups: employers looking for technology talent, candidates looking for their next role, and students building technical skills.

Training runs online and in person. Certificates are issued on successful completion and can be verified through a public reference.

Our staffing work covers contract, contract-to-hire and direct placement. Every engagement starts with a defined role and an agreed process.
