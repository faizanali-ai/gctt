Headquartered in Irving, Texas, Global Center for Technology & Training operates at the nexus of talent strategy and corporate technology demands. We serve three distinct cohorts: enterprises scaling their technical capacity, candidates seeking vetted roles, and professionals upskilling in high-demand enterprise software.

Our hybrid delivery model combines online and instructor-led cohorts in SAP functional modules and Agile frameworks, backed by digitally verifiable credentials.
