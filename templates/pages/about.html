Operating from our corporate headquarters at 2612 Laramie St in Irving, Texas, Global Center for Technology & Training (GCTekton) was established to solve a fundamental enterprise dilemma: the widening chasm between superficial keyword-matching agencies and the rigorous architectural reality of modern engineering organizations.

Rather than functioning as a transactional resume broker, GCTekton operates an integrated talent capability hub. Every candidate we submit has undergone rigorous technical evaluation by practicing senior architects. Concurrently, our proprietary academy delivers intensive, sandbox-driven cohorts in mission-critical platforms—including SAP S/4HANA (MM, FICO, SD) and Scaled Agile enterprise delivery.

Our dual-track infrastructure guarantees that our corporate partners receive day-one delivery velocity, backed by uncompromising right-to-work compliance, E-Verify verification, and transparent engagement frameworks across all 50 states.
