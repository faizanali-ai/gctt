Operating from our corporate headquarters at 2612 Laramie St in Irving, Texas, Global Center for Technology & Training (GCTT) was established to solve a fundamental enterprise dilemma: the widening chasm between superficial keyword-matching agencies and the rigorous architectural reality of modern engineering organizations.

Rather than functioning as a transactional resume broker, GCTT operates an integrated talent capability hub. Every candidate we submit has undergone rigorous technical evaluation by practicing senior architects. Concurrently, our proprietary academy delivers intensive, sandbox-driven cohorts in mission-critical platforms—including SAP S/4HANA (MM, FICO, SD) and Scaled Agile enterprise delivery.

Our dual-track infrastructure guarantees that our corporate partners receive day-one delivery velocity, backed by uncompromising right-to-work compliance, E-Verify verification, and transparent engagement frameworks across all 50 states.

Operational Architecture

Our three foundational pillars

Designed to deliver complete organizational resilience, technical capability, and verified talent mobility.

Pillar I • Staffing Governance

Enterprise Talent Augmentation

Deploying elite software engineers, solution architects, and technical scrum leads via direct W2, Statement of Work (SOW), and contract-to-hire frameworks with pre-screened technical rigor.

Pillar II • Workforce Academy

Hands-On Technical Academy

Immersive, sandbox-driven training curricula in SAP S/4HANA functional modules and Agile frameworks. Every graduate is awarded a digitally verifiable, tamper-evident credential.

Pillar III • Talent Mobility

Executive Returnship Accelerator

A dedicated program engineered to transition seasoned technology leaders and specialists back into senior enterprise roles following career pauses, backed by modern toolchain re-skilling.
